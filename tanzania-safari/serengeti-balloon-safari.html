Serengeti Balloon Safari

Imagine floating above the vast plains of the Serengeti National Park, with the sun rising over endless golden grasses and the majestic Mount Kilimanjaro piercing the clouds in the distance. This is the magic of a Serengeti balloon safari, an unforgettable experience that will leave you feeling breathless with awe.

What to Expect on a Serengeti Balloon Safari:

This unique adventure offers a perspective of the Serengeti that cannot be replicated on any other safari. As you soar through the air, you will be treated to breathtaking panoramic views of the landscape, witnessing the incredible diversity of wildlife roaming freely below.

Early Morning Start: 

 Your journey begins at dawn, when the air is still and the sun casts a soft glow over the landscape. You will be met by your expert pilot and crew, who will help you climb into the balloon basket and ensure your safety.

Gentle Ascent: 

As the pilot ignites the burners, the balloon will begin to rise slowly and gracefully into the sky. The feeling of weightlessness as you leave the ground is truly exhilarating.

Unparalleled Views: 

Once you reach cruising altitude, you will be mesmerized by the breathtaking panorama unfolding beneath you. Rolling hills, endless plains, and shimmering watering holes will stretch out before your eyes, teeming with diverse wildlife.

Wildlife Encounters:

 From herds of wildebeest and zebra migrating across the plains to elephants lumbering through the savannah and lions resting in the shade of acacia trees, you will have the opportunity to witness an incredible array of animals from a unique aerial perspective.

Bush Breakfast: 

After landing, you will be treated to a delicious bush breakfast in the heart of the wilderness. Enjoy your meal surrounded by the sights and sounds of the African bush, sharing stories and reliving the highlights of your flight.

Benefits of a Serengeti Balloon Safari:

Unique Perspective: 

A balloon safari offers a completely different perspective of the Serengeti compared to traditional game drives. You will be able to see vast distances and gain a deeper understanding of the interconnectedness of the ecosystem.

Access to Remote Areas: 

Balloons can access areas that are difficult or impossible to reach by vehicle, allowing you to witness hidden corners of the Serengeti and encounter less-common wildlife.

Minimal Environmental Impact: 

Hot air balloons have minimal impact on the environment, making them a sustainable and eco-friendly way to experience the Serengeti.

Unforgettable Experience: 

A Serengeti balloon safari is a truly unforgettable experience that will create lasting memories for years to come.

Serengeti Balloon Safari - FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions About Serengeti Balloon Safari

The best time to go on a Serengeti Balloon Safari is during the dry season, which runs from June to October. During this time, the skies are clear, the weather is pleasant, and the animals are concentrated around watering holes.

The cost of a Serengeti Balloon Safari varies depending on the length of the flight, and the time of year. However, you can expect to pay between $500 and $1,000 per person.

It's important to wear comfortable clothing that is suitable for the weather. You'll also want to wear shoes that are comfortable for walking, as you may need to walk a short distance to the launch site.

You should bring a camera, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat, and a light jacket. You may also want to bring a pair of binoculars and a small backpack to carry your belongings.

Hot air ballooning is strictly regulated in Tanzania, with operators required to adhere to rigorous safety standards. All pilots must be licensed and experienced, and all balloons undergo regular inspections.

We offer combination packages that include both a balloon safari and a traditional game drive. This is a great way to experience the Serengeti from both ground and air perspectives.

If the weather conditions are not safe for ballooning, the operator will cancel the flight and offer you a full refund or reschedule your flight for another day.
