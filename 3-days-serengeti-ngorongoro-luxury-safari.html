3-Days Serengeti and Ngorongoro Luxury Safari

Overview

This 3-day safari gives you a chance to witness amazing animals and sleep in luxury accommodations. With Serengeti Smile, you will be immersed in the unspoiled nature of real Africa. You will see wild animals up close and take photos of the wonderful landscapes of Tanzania.

All this with the assistance of a Tour Operator based in Arusha, full board on safaris, personal English-speaking guide, and private use 4x4 Jeep (with mineral water, power sockets, binoculars).

  1. Day 1:

    Arusha to Serengeti National Park

    You'll be picked up from your lodge and depart to Serengeti National Park for game drives while carrying lunch boxes. Once we finish the game drive, we will transfer you to the lodge for relaxation, dinner and an overnight stay.

    Serengeti is more well-known for its great migrating herds of zebra, antelope, and wildebeest. Be ready to see resident predators, leopard, cheetah, and lion, fulfilling their role in the circle of life.

    Main Destination: Serengeti National Park

    Accommodation: Serengeti Serena Safari Lodge

    • Meals & Drinks: Dinner (Breakfast & lunch not included)
  2. Day 2:

    Serengeti National Park to Ngorongoro for Overnight on the Rim

    Today, wake up early and enjoy magic game drives in Serengeti National Park. Then in the afternoon, depart to Ngorongoro for an overnight at the accommodation located on the Ngorongoro Crater Rim. This gives you chance to descend inside the Crater the next day in the early morning and see many animals when they are still more active.

    Serengeti is more well-known for its great migrating herds of zebra, antelope, and wildebeest. Be ready to see resident predators including leopard, cheetah, and lion, fulfilling their role in the circle of life.

    Main Destination: Serengeti National Park

    Accommodation: Ngorongoro Serena Safari Lodge

    • Meals & Drinks: All Meals Included
  3. Day 3:

    Ngorongoro Crater to Arusha Town

    After breakfast, we'll descend inside Ngorongoro Crater for game drives. Once we finish the game drives, we will transfer you to Arusha where we will finish our safari.

    This park constitutes the largest uninterrupted caldera in the world and one of the Natural Wonders of Africa.

    Main Destination: Ngorongoro Crater

    Accommodation: No Accommodation
