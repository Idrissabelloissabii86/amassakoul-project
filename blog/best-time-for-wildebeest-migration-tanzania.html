When is The Best Time To See Wildebeest Migration?

Discover when is the best time to see Wildebeest Migration in Serengeti,Tanzania. Explore weather patterns, wildlife sightings, and insider tips for planning your unforgettable safari.

The Great Wildebeest Migration in Tanzania

The Great Wildebeest Migration is one of the most spectacular natural events in the world, and Tanzania is the best place to see it. Over two million wildebeest, along with hundreds of thousands of zebra and gazelle, make a circular journey of over 3,000 kilometers (1,800 miles) each year in search of water and grazing land.

The Great Wildebeest Migration Seasons in Tanzania

The best time to see the wildebeest migration in Tanzania depends on what you want to see. It is possible to look at historic migration patterns to predict where the herds will be at a particular time. You can witness the Great Wildebeest Migration at any time of year.

Here is a breakdown of the different season of the Great Migration:

Location Highlights
Calving season (January - March) Southern Serengeti, Ndutu Plains Witness the birth of thousands of wildebeest calves, enjoy excellent predator sightings as lions and cheetahs target the vulnerable newborns.
Green season (April - May) Southern Serengeti, Ndutu Plains, moving towards the central Serengeti Lush green landscapes, wildebeest herds grazing on the fresh grass, fewer crowds compared to peak season.
The build-up (June - July) Western Serengeti, Grumeti River The herds start their northward journey, witness the first river crossings at the Grumeti River, dramatic encounters with crocodiles.
Peak season (August - October) Northern Serengeti, Mara River The most dramatic and well-known part of the migration, witness the wildebeest thundering across the Mara River, experience the peak of predator activity.
Southern return (November - December) Eastern Serengeti, moving back towards the south The herds start their southward journey, witness the vast plains teeming with wildlife, enjoy comfortable weather conditions.
Wildebeest Migration Season in Tanzania

So, when is the best time to go?

The best time to see the wildebeest migration in Tanzania really depends on what you want to see. If you want to see the calving season, then December to March is the best time. If you want to see the dramatic river crossings, then June to October is the best time. And if you want to see the herds on the move, then April to May or November to December is the best time.

No matter when you decide to go, seeing the wildebeest migration in Tanzania is an unforgettable experience. With its stunning scenery, incredible wildlife, and dramatic events, it is truly one of the wonders of the natural world.
