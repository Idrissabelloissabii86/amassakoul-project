Serengeti Smile Safari Vehicles

Our Safari Vehicles are all Toyota Land-cruiser 4x4s with pop-up roofs. We ensure that each vehicle is meticulously serviced and maintained to the highest standard before, during, and after every safari adventure.

Our experienced guides ensure a safety and comfortable safari experience in the African wilderness, handling any situation that may arise.

Our safari vehicles are equipped with amenities like:

  1. Fridge: for cooling your drinks and snacks

  2. Binoculars: for wildlife viewing

  3. Charging Stations: for your electronic devices, ie;- Camera, Phones, e.t.c

  4. Luggage Storage

  5. Window Seat

  6. Pop-up roofs: For easy game viewing

We strive to provide the best safari experience possible for our guests.
