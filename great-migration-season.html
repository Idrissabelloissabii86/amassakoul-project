Migration Calving Season

When: January to April

Each year, between January and March, the southern Serengeti plains transform into a birthing ground for the Great Migration herds. Over 500,000 wildebeest calves are born during this short period, creating incredible viewing opportunities. The calving season not only brings new life but also attracts predators, leading to captivating wildlife interactions.

Why Visit During Calving Season?

The calving season offers a rare chance to see the Serengeti in a new light—lush, green landscapes filled with young animals and thrilling predator-prey dynamics. It's an ideal time for photographers, wildlife enthusiasts, and travelers looking for a quieter yet highly engaging safari experience.

  • Witness Thousands of Newborns

  • Incredible Predator Sightings

  • Scenic Beauty

Migration Crossing Season

When: July to October

Between July and October, the Great Migration reaches its most dramatic phase as millions of wildebeest, zebras, and antelope cross the Mara and Grumeti Rivers, facing daunting obstacles like fast currents and predatory crocodiles. These river crossings are among nature’s most thrilling spectacles, drawing travelers from around the globe.

Why Experience the Crossing Season?

The crossing season offers unparalleled excitement, with breathtaking scenes of determination, survival, and the sheer spectacle of vast herds attempting these daring river crossings.

  • Witness Dramatic River Crossings

  • High Predator Activity

  • Unique Photography
