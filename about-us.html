About Us

WHY US?

We understand that when our guests visit Tanzania on a safari, holiday, or honeymoon, it is often a lifetime dream and normally a major expense. We are therefore here to ensure that you receive real, expert advice.

We want to ensure, that based on your interests, budget, and season of travel, we select the correct destinations, lodges, camps, and hotels for your itinerary.

SERENGETI SMILE

SERENGETI SMILE is the Best Tour Operator organizing Wildlife Safaris, Beach Holidays, Mountains Trekking, and Cultural Tours in Tanzania and East Africa in General. We organize highly professional Safaris and Holidays to the most magnificent destinations offering an amazing experience for a lifetime.

Over the years, we have taken the time to travel extensively throughout Tanzania’s most loved wildlife areas. In addition, we have arranged hundreds of safaris and holidays for thousands of guests, and we, therefore, believe that we are highly qualified to assist you through the process of arranging and booking your dream holiday to Tanzania.

Our high-quality service, convivial and knowledgeable guides, professional attention, and very good safari vehicles may be taken for granted. However, our edge over the others in the field is personal warmth and we always give your dreams and budgets a priority to guarantee your pleasure. Join us for an extremely exhilarating African experience – a dose of adventure, peace, comfort, relaxation, and fun.

What We Do

We offer you the opportunity to explore East Africa’s vastness, which includes a fine selection of spectacular mountains, National Parks, and wilderness areas presenting unsurpassable natural beauty and a chance to discover unexplored Africa. This region of the African subcontinent offers a balance of natural heritage and cultural diversity.

The thrills of climbing the majestic snow-clad Mount Kilimanjaro or the rocky peaks of Mount Kenya or visiting the endless plains of the Serengeti, the world-famous and wildlife-rich Masai Mara, the awesome Ngorongoro crater, and tracking the gorillas in Uganda and Rwanda. These constitute a fraction of the intriguing splendors of East Africa. It all adds up to an immeasurably valuable experience found nowhere else in the world.
