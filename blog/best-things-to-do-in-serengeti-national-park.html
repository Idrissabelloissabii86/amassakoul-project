What Are The Best Things to Do in Serengeti?

Serengeti National Park is one of the most iconic wildlife destinations in the world. From breathtaking landscapes to incredible wildlife encounters, it offers an adventure like no other. Here are the top 10 best things to do in Serengeti National Park to make your trip unforgettable.

Top 10 Best Things to Do in Serengeti National Park

1. Witness the Great Migration

The Great Migration is one of nature’s most spectacular events, where millions of wildebeest, zebras, and other herbivores move across the Serengeti in search of fresh grazing.

When to See the Migration

The best time to witness the Great Migration is from July to October, when the herds cross the Mara River. This period offers thrilling scenes of animals braving crocodile-infested waters.

Where to See the Migration

The Western Corridor and Northern Serengeti are best places to catch the migration. These areas provide excellent viewpoints for observing the herds as they move.

2. Experience a Hot Air Balloon Safari

Floating above the Serengeti in a hot air balloon offers a unique perspective of the park's vast landscapes and wildlife.

What to Expect on a Balloon Safari

A balloon safari typically starts early in the morning, providing stunning sunrise views. You’ll glide over the plains, spotting wildlife from above, and conclude with a champagne breakfast upon landing.

Best Time for Balloon Safaris

The dry season, from June to October, is ideal for balloon safaris. The weather is more predictable, and wildlife is easier to spot.

3. Enjoy a Classic Game Drive

A game drive is the quintessential way to explore the Serengeti. With a knowledgeable guide, you’ll venture into different regions of the park to see a diverse array of animals.

Tips for a Successful Game Drive

Start early in the morning or late in the afternoon when animals are most active. Bring binoculars, wear neutral-coloured clothing, and carry plenty of water.

Animals to Spot

Look out for the Big Five (lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, and rhino), as well as cheetahs, giraffes, and a variety of antelope species.

4. Visit a Maasai Village

Visiting a Maasai village offers a cultural experience, allowing you to learn about the traditions and lifestyle of the Maasai people.

Understanding Maasai Culture

The Maasai are known for their distinctive customs and dress. They live in harmony with nature, maintaining a semi-nomadic lifestyle that revolves around cattle herding.

What to Expect During the Visit

You’ll have the opportunity to participate in traditional dances, visit a Maasai home, and learn about their daily life and rituals.

5. Explore the Serengeti on Foot

For a more intimate experience, consider a guided walking safari. This allows you to appreciate the smaller details of the ecosystem that are often missed from a vehicle.

Guided Walking Safaris

These safaris are led by experienced guides who ensure your safety while providing insights into the flora and fauna.

Safety Tips

Always follow your guide’s instructions, wear comfortable walking shoes, and stay aware of your surroundings.

6. Discover the Moru Kopjes

The Moru Kopjes are ancient rock formations scattered across the Serengeti. They are not only geologically fascinating but also rich in wildlife.

Wildlife Around the Kopjes

Lions often use the kopjes as vantage points, and you might also spot elephants, rhinos, and various bird species.

History and Rock Art

The Moru Kopjes are home to Maasai rock paintings and ancient petroglyphs, providing a glimpse into the region’s history.

7. Capture Stunning Photographs

Serengeti offers endless opportunities for photography, from expansive landscapes to close-up wildlife shots.

Best Spots for Photography

Best locations include the Seronera Valley, the Mara River, and the Moru Kopjes. These areas provide diverse settings and abundant wildlife.

Photography Tips

Use a telephoto lens for close-ups, be patient, and take advantage of the golden hours (early morning and late afternoon) for the best light.

8. Spend a Night in a Luxury Lodge

For a truly indulgent experience, stay in one of Serengeti’s luxury lodges. These accommodations offer comfort and excellent service in the heart of the wilderness.
Top Luxury Lodges in Serengeti

Some of the top lodges include Four Seasons Safari Lodge, Serengeti Bushtops, and Singita Grumeti. Each offers unique amenities and stunning views.

What to Expect at a Luxury Lodge

Expect spacious rooms, gourmet dining, and opportunities for exclusive game drives and guided walks. Many lodges also offer spa services and infinity pools.

9. Bird Watching

Serengeti is a birdwatcher’s paradise, with over 500 bird species recorded in the park.

Best Bird Watching Locations

The Seronera Valley and the Grumeti River are excellent spots for bird watching. These areas attract a variety of species due to their water sources and diverse habitats.

Bird Species to Look Out For

Keep an eye out for the Kori bustard, Secretary bird, and a variety of raptors, including eagles and vultures.

10. Visit the Serengeti Visitor Centre

The Visitor Centre offers educational exhibits and information about the park’s history and conservation efforts.

What You’ll Learn at the Visitor Centre

Learn about the Serengeti’s ecosystem, wildlife, and the ongoing efforts to protect this unique environment.

Activities and Exhibits

The centre features informative displays, guided walks, and sometimes film screenings. It’s a great place to start your Serengeti adventure.

Conclusion

Serengeti National Park is a treasure trove of experiences waiting to be discovered. Whether you’re seeking adventure, relaxation, or cultural insights, the Serengeti has something for everyone. Don’t miss out on these top activities to make the most of your visit.
