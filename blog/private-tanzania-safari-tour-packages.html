Top 6 Private Tanzania Safari Tour Packages for Unforgettable Wildlife Holidays 2025/2026

Think of hearing a lion’s roar in the distance as the acacia trees rustle softly and golden sunlight spills over the open savannah. No, this isn't a wildlife documentary this is just another day on safari in Tanzania.

If you've ever dreamt of spotting the Big Five, witnessing the iconic Great Wildebeest Migration, or escaping into one of the last untouched wildernesses on Earth, then it’s time to explore the Best Private Tanzania Safari Tour Packages.

Whether you’re planning a romantic honeymoon, a family trip, or a personal journey of discovery, Tanzania offers an extraordinary blend of adventure, serenity, and wild beauty. From the vast plains of the Serengeti to the awe-inspiring Ngorongoro Crater, we’ve handpicked six private safari experiences that promise memories to last a lifetime.

Featured Safari Tours You’ll Love

Here are some of the most popular and carefully designed private Tanzania safari tour packages available through trusted local operators:

1. Amazing Serengeti and Ngorongoro Safari – 3 Days

This package is the perfect introduction to Tanzania’s northern safari circuit. Beginning in Arusha, it takes you across the wildlife-rich Serengeti plains, and deep into the Ngorongoro Crater.

Best For: First-time safari travellers and couples
Highlights: Big Five sightings, luxury tented camps, crater floor game drives

2. The Great Migration Tour at Mara River Crossing – 7 Days

A Tanzanian safari is incomplete without witnessing the Great Migration. This 7-day Private Tanzania safari tour follows the seasonal path of thousands of wildebeests and zebras as they move across the Serengeti toward the Mara River. With a private guide by your side, you'll experience nature’s most dramatic theater up close and without the crowd.


Best For: Wildlife photographers and nature lovers
Highlights: Predator viewing, river crossings, open savannah landscapes

3. Luxury Tanzania Safari – 4 Days

A luxurious escape through Tanzania’s most iconic national parks. With upscale lodges, personal guides, and fine dining, this is a seamless blend of convenience and luxury.

Best For: Honeymooners and high-end travellers
Highlights: Scenic flights, exclusive bush dinners, sundowner experiences

4. Bush & Beach Combo – 10 Days

Adventure followed by relaxation, what could be better? Start your journey in the wild, exploring Tarangire, Serengeti, and Ngorongoro, before flying to the white-sand beaches of Zanzibar. This tour offers the best of both worlds. The ultimate bush-and-beach experience for a perfect blend of adventure and relaxation.

Best For: Couples, honeymooners, and relaxation seekers
Highlights: Elephant sightings, spice tours, coral reef snorkeling

5. Tanzania Safari Adventure – 5 Days

A well-rounded classic safari ideal for first-time visitors. This takes you  through Tanzania’s most iconic national parks. With upscale lodges, personal guides, and fine dining

Ideal For: Active explorers and bucket-list adventurers
Highlights: safari game drives, optional hot air balloon

6. Amazing Wildlife and Cultural Experience Tour – 6 Days

Combines game drives with immersive cultural activities like Maasai village visits.
Best For: Families with children aged 6 and above
Highlights: Safe game drives, educational activities, Maasai storytelling

With a private guide and vehicle, each experience is tailored to your interests. No fixed schedules. No large groups. Just you, nature, and the adventure of a lifetime.
