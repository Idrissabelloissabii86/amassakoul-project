Tanzania vs Kenya Safari: Which is Better For an African Safari?

Kenya and Tanzania are two of the most popular safari destinations in Africa. Both countries offer stunning scenery, diverse wildlife, and a variety of safari experiences. But which country is the better choice for your African safari?

Tanzania

Tanzania is a great choice for travelers who are looking for a more remote and less crowded safari experience. The country is home to the Serengeti National Park, the largest wildlife reserve in Africa. The Serengeti is known for its vast plains, which are home to a wide variety of animals, including lions, elephants, zebras, and giraffes. Tanzania is also home to other popular safari destinations such as the Ngorongoro Crater, Lake Manyara National Park, and Tarangire National Park.

Kenya

Kenya is a great choice for travelers who are looking for a classic African safari experience. The country is home to the Masai Mara National Reserve, one of the most famous wildlife reserves in the world. The Masai Mara is known for its annual wildebeest migration, which is one of the most spectacular natural events on earth. Kenya is also home to other popular safari destinations such as Amboseli National Park, Lake Nakuru National Park, and Aberdare National Park.

Which is Better For an African Safari?

The best country for your African safari depends on your individual preferences and priorities. If you are looking for a classic African safari experience with plenty of wildlife viewing, Kenya is a great choice. If you are looking for a more remote and less crowded safari experience, Tanzania is a great choice.

Tanzania vs Kenya Safari: Factors to Consider

Here are some of the factors to consider when deciding between a Kenya safari and a Tanzania safari:

Wildlife:

Both Kenya and Tanzania safari offer a wide variety of wildlife, but some animals are more prevalent in one country than the other. For example, Kenya is home to more rhinos and black leopards, while Tanzania is home to more lions and elephants.

Scenery: 

Both Kenya and Tanzania safari offer stunning scenery, but the landscapes are different. Kenya is known for its rolling hills and savannas, while Tanzania is known for its vast plains and volcanic craters.

Crowds: 

Kenya is a more popular tourist destination than Tanzania, so it can be more crowded, especially during peak season. If you are looking for a more secluded safari experience, Tanzania is a better choice.

Budget: 

Safaris in Kenya are generally less expensive than safaris in Tanzania.

Tanzania vs Kenya Safari Cost:

The cost of a safari can vary depending on a number of factors, including the length of the safari, the type of accommodation, and the time of year. However, in general, safaris in Kenya are less expensive than safaris in Tanzania. This is because Kenya has a more developed tourist infrastructure and a wider range of accommodation options.

Safety:

Tanzania and Kenya are both safe countries to travel to, but it is important to take precautions to protect yourself from petty crime. When choosing a safari company, it is important to make sure that they are reputable and that they have a good safety record.

Best time to go:

The best time to go on a safari in Tanzania is during the dry season, which is from June to October. The best time to go on a safari in Kenya is during the dry season, which is from July to November.
